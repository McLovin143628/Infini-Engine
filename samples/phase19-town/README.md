# Phase 19 Town (the phase gate scene)

Generated by `inf_editor_core::samples::phase19_town_scene` -- the **composed**
gate scene for Phase 19 (biomes, PCG grammar & enterable structures).

- `Phase19Town.inf_lvl` -- a **partitioned** level (128 m cells) over a four-tile
biome-painted `Terrain`, a `Spline` road with the `PcgVolume` that fences it,
**seven building lots (one per archetype)**, a walker that is the streaming
source, a `TimeOfDay` + `SkyAtmosphere` authority, a sun and a camera.
- `Town.inf_biomes` -- two biomes (Town / Meadow); the strip within 90 m of the
road is painted Town, everything beyond it Meadow.
- `Roadside.inf_pcg` -- a `grammar.rules` fence expanded along the road actor's
own spline, merged with a light verge scatter. Every fence module declares a
`collider`, so the fence is a barrier rather than a picture of one.
- `Lot<Archetype>.inf_pcg` (x7) -- `building.archetype -> building.plan ->
output.pcg`, one per palette: Office, Apartment, Industrial, House, Estate,
Hotel, Shop.

## Why one volume per lot

A building's footprint is its volume's own box -- the same default a
`grammar.footprint` span has. Seven archetypes on one canvas would need a per-node
lot offset, which is the volume's transform spelt a second way. So the level reads
as what it is: seven plots on a street, each with its own tiny graph.

## Why the terrain is authored inline, not streamed from an `.inf_terrain`

Phase 16 already gates heightfield tile streaming end to end
(`phase16_gate`, `streamed_terrain`). What Phase 19 needs from streaming is that
**buildings respect cells**, which is the *entity* partition -- so this level turns
world partition on with a deliberately small 128 m cell (half the engine default)
and authors its 512 m heightfield inline. Sixteen cells over the world means the
seven lots land in more than one of them, which is what makes the binning
assertion non-vacuous; a level whose every building sat in cell (0,0) would prove
nothing.

## Why the relief is gentle

A building levels its site: `base_y` is sampled once, at the footprint centre, and
every slab, wall and stair shares that datum. phase18's ~40 m of relief over 512 m
exists to put scatter behind occluders; here it would post a 44 m lot on stilts.
~6 m over 512 m is enough to prove the terrain is read and not enough to lift a
floor slab off the ground.

## The buildings are derived, so the level stays small

`PcgVolume.evaluated` and `PcgVolume.structures` are both `#[serde(skip)]`: the
instances **and the colliders** are recomputed on load from the committed
`.inf_pcg` (editor `pcg_evaluate`, shipped/PIE player `evaluate_pcg_volumes`).
Seven fully furnished three-storey buildings cost the `.inf_lvl` nothing.

## The gate (`runtime/inf-player/tests/phase19_gate.rs`)

1. full-trace determinism across two fresh loads (population + building solids +
partition residency);
2. cooked == uncooked, bit for bit;
3. PIE == shipping, bit for bit, on the placed set;
4. **enterability** -- for one building per archetype: the room graph is connected,
every door opening's rect holds no collider, and every floor is reachable from
OUTSIDE by a graph walk through the entrance, the doors and the stair cores;
5. the lots bin into the partition cells their transforms say they do;
6. the composed scene loads inside the frame budget.

Regenerate with `INF_BLESS_SAMPLES=1 cargo test -p inf-editor-core samples`.
