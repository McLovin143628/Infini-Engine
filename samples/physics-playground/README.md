# Physics Playground (Phase-12 gate scene)

Generated by `inf_editor_core::samples::physics_playground_scene` — the P12.4 gate
scene: a committed 3D physics playground composing every P12 feature at once — a
settling **box stack**, a **motorized revolute spinner**, a **distance-rope
pendulum**, a **prismatic slider**, a **CCD bullet** vs a thin wall, a
**collision-layer ghost pair** (overlapping, non-interacting via filters), a
**sensor plate**, and a small **ragdoll** (`inf_physics::ragdoll::build_ragdoll`
output, its joint descs mapped to `Joint3D` components) — plus two spatial
**AudioSource**s (one autoplay-looping on the spinner with occlusion, one on the
sensor) and an **AudioListener** on a camera.

- `Playground.inf_lvl` — the scene as schema-**v6** `.inf_lvl` bytes (joints +
audio persist).
- `Spinner.inf_audio` / `Sensor.inf_audio` — the two clips the AudioSources
reference (a short deterministic tone; shipped via the cook's level→audio edge).

Determinism is asserted by `runtime/inf-player/tests/physics_demo.rs`: 300 fixed
steps twice yield a byte-identical pose trace (xxh3 over Guid-sorted transforms)
AND an identical audio command stream; the ragdoll settles bounded, the CCD bullet
is stopped, the ghost pair interpenetrates unimpeded, PIE == shipping.

Regenerate with `INF_BLESS_SAMPLES=1 cargo test -p inf-editor-core samples`.
