# Phase 16 World (the phase gate scene)

Generated by `inf_editor_core::samples::phase16_world_scene` -- the **composed**
gate scene for Phase 16 (world scale & streaming). Everything the phase built, in
one level.

- `Phase16World.inf_lvl` -- the scene (schema v10). A partitioned 4x4 grid of 2 km
cells with a prop in each, a `GameMode` with no spatial component, an
`AlwaysLoaded` sun and camera, a `Walker` that is BOTH the cell-streaming
`StreamingSource` and the terrain observer, and **two terrains**.
- `Phase16World.inf_terrain` -- NOT committed. ~5 MB of derived bytes that
`samples::write_phase16_terrain_asset` reproduces exactly by running the P16.4a
**chunked importer** over a synthetic 1025^2 16-bit heightmap at 8 m/sample:
8x8 level-0 pages of 129^2 samples over 8.2 km of world, plus two coarse pyramid
levels (64 -> 16 -> 4).

## The two terrains

`Terrain` streams from the `.inf_terrain`; `Inline Terrain` carries its tiles in
the level, on a completely different grid (9^2 samples at 4 m) and at a different
world origin. Both render, both are picked against, and both survive the cook --
which is P16.6's multi-terrain deliverable end to end.

Both are marked `AlwaysLoaded`. A `Terrain` component *occupies space*
(`inf_scene::partition::occupies_space`), so without the marker the partitioner
would bin the terrain entity into the ONE cell holding its origin -- and the ground
would despawn under the player as they walked out of it. That is a v1 boundary of
world partition, and the cook says so: `partition::streamed_terrains` raises an
advisory naming the terrain, its cell, its `.inf_terrain` and the remedy, so a user
authoring their own partitioned level is told rather than left to discover it.
Binning a terrain by its real footprint instead of its origin is the deferred fix.

## Scale, honestly

The Phase 16 goal names a 16k x 16k source. That is ~268 M samples and ~1 GB of
payload however it is tiled, so it cannot run in a CI job that also cooks it into
a pack -- the *shape* of the import here is the real one (chunked row-band decode,
banded pyramid, 8 m spacing, kilometre-scale extent) at a size CI can afford. The
literal 16k pass is `terrain_import::huge_heightmap_16k_imports`, `#[ignore]`d and
run by hand when the importer is profiled.

## The gate (`runtime/inf-player/tests/phase16_gate.rs`)

1. full determinism across two runs -- sim trace, cell activation timeline, and
terrain render-cut trace;
2. cooked == uncooked (PIE == shipping) on all three traces, with TWO terrains
projected;
3. residency stays under the configured ceilings (`inf_player::budget`) for the
whole run -- terrain bytes, cell bytes, active cells;
4. the fixed-step ms budget holds while both streamers are live;
5. the sim trace is invariant under the camera path AND under the prefetch
margin -- the composed doctrine proof: neither the renderer's residency nor
the streamer's look-ahead can reach a fixed step.

Regenerate with `INF_BLESS_SAMPLES=1 cargo test -p inf-editor-core samples`.
