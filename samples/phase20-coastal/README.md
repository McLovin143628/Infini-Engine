# Phase 20 Coastal (the phase gate scene)

Generated by `inf_editor_core::samples::phase20_coastal_scene` -- the **composed**
gate scene for Phase 20 (water & hydrology), and the plan's own done-when sentence
built: *an ocean plus a spline river fed by a lake, carrying buoyant physics
objects.*

- `Phase20Coastal.inf_lvl` -- a 512 m coastal heightfield (a 42 m headland falling
to -10 m, with a meandering valley and a dug basin), an **ocean** at sea level, a
**head lake** in the basin, a **spline river** running the valley from the lake to
the shore with a 6 -> 14 m width taper and a 1.2 -> 2.0 m depth taper, **eight
buoyant crates** (six at sea, two on the lake) and a **swimmer**.
- `Swimmer.inf_act` -- one Tick calling `physics3d.move_and_slide` with a brisk
forward request and a full second of accumulated free fall, so the committed
content exercises the swim mode's asymmetric sink authority rather than only a
unit test doing so.

## Why the terrain is authored inline

Phase 16 gates heightfield tile streaming end to end. What Phase 20 needs from the
ground is a *shoreline* -- somewhere the sea meets land, so shore blending, the
wetness band and the river's mouth all appear in one scene -- and 512 m of inline
heightfield is the cheapest way to have one.

## Why the height function is a polynomial

`std` trigonometry is not bit-portable (the P14 LAW), and this is COMMITTED
content: the terrain has to author identically on every machine or the `.inf_lvl`
would not round-trip byte-for-byte. The meander is a cubic, the valley walls are a
quadratic and the basin is a paraboloid -- IEEE add/mul throughout.

## Why the river's numbers are monotone

Both cook advisories -- the P20.1 surface climb and the P20.4 authored-bed climb --
must be SILENT on this scene, and the gate asserts it. An advisory that fires on
the engine's own flagship sample is one nobody reads. The surface descends from
~33.4 m to ~1.0 m and the depth taper widens downstream, so the bed descends too.

## The gate (`runtime/inf-player/tests/phase20_gate.rs`)

1. determinism -- two fresh loads of one pack agree on the whole physics trace,
bit for bit;
2. **PIE == shipping** -- the cooked-pack world and the PIE-payload world float the
same crates and swim the same swimmer;
3. the water is really doing something (crates settle at their draughts, the
swimmer surfaces, the river's mouth is finite);
4. the cook is silent -- no water advisory on correct content;
5. budget -- the composed scene builds inside the load budget and steps inside the
frame budget.
