# Phase 21 Cavern (the phase gate scene)

Generated by `inf_editor_core::samples::phase21_cavern_scene` -- the **composed**
gate scene for Phase 21 (volumetric terrain), and the plan's own done-when
sentence built: *on a streamed terrain, carve a cave system and excavate a
foundation pit with displaced soil piles, build an underground room in the pit,
save and reload byte-identical, and it works in PIE.*

- `Phase21Cavern.inf_lvl` -- a 128 m ridge (2 x 2 tiles at 2 m/sample) with an
**asset-backed** terrain, one `VoxelVolume` entity carrying the rock body and
the borer Blueprint, a lamp standing on the underground room's floor, and a
sky/sun/camera.
- `Phase21Cavern.inf_terrain` -- the heightfield **and its hole mask**. The mask
is why the terrain is asset-backed rather than inline: scene schema v19's
frozen tile record has no hole rows, so an inline terrain cannot persist one
and saving would seal every cave mouth.
- `Cavern.inf_voxel` -- eight 16 m SDF chunks of rock, with the cave system, the
foundation pit, the underground room + its shaft, and the pit's **spoil heap**
cut into them. The heap holds exactly the pit's per-material voxel count, with
no bulking factor: a 1:1 identity is a gate, a 1.25x fudge is a number nobody
can test.
- `Borer.inf_act` -- the runtime-carving Blueprint. Every Tick it advances 0.5 m
along +Z and carves a 2 m ball with `voxel.carve_sphere`, accumulating the
cubic metres removed and recording both ground queries. It runs on **Tick**
rather than BeginPlay because both hosts seed their voxel map after building
the sim, so a BeginPlay carve would find no volume.

Exercised by `runtime/inf-player/tests/phase21_gate.rs`.

Regenerate with `INF_BLESS_SAMPLES=1 cargo test -p inf-editor-core samples`.
