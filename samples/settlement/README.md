# The settlement zone library (island wave I8a)

Generated by `inf_editor_core::settlement::write_settlement_library`. **Seven
`.inf_pcg` documents, one per building archetype**, each a few hundred bytes of
RULES: a footprint span over the evaluating volume's own extent, IB-2c's
`building.lots` subdivision, and a `building.plan` on the archetype's palette.

They are ENGINE content, like `samples/ground` and `samples/starter-character`:
one copy, byte-locked against the generator, named by both islands' recipes
under `[content]` and by every settlement block's `PcgVolume.graph`.

## Why seven documents and not one per block

A committed level carries one `PcgVolume` record per block -- a centre, a
half-extent, a seed and a graph GUID. What makes a hundred and seventy volumes a
hundred and seventy different blocks is the **seed**, not a hundred and seventy
graphs. Committing a document per block would be committing geometry where the
small-committed-folder law says commit rules.

## The zoning table lives in the generator, not here

Which archetype a block takes is `inf_editor_core::settlement::zone_table` --
a deterministic function of the block's ring and its site's kind, plus one
industrial cluster per city near the highway. A zone document knows nothing
about where it is used.

Regenerate with `INF_BLESS_SAMPLES=1 cargo test -p inf-editor-core samples`.
